Chapter 1: About the opportunity

The Mastercard Foundation Scholars Program at the University of Cape Town (UCT) supports academically talented young Africans to pursue undergraduate and postgraduate studies.

It is designed for innovative, purpose-driven applicants who are committed to leading positive social and economic change in Africa. The programme combines university study with personal, professional and leadership development.

This is not only a funding opportunity. Scholars are expected to take part in community-upliftment projects and engage actively in the wider UCT experience.

Chapter 2: What Scholars receive

The programme provides a scholarship alongside developmental opportunities that help students succeed at university and prepare for impact after graduation.

  • Support to pursue an undergraduate or postgraduate qualification at UCT.
  • Mentoring and leadership-development opportunities.
  • Academic and life-skills support.
  • Career-development support.
  • Entrepreneurship training.
  • Opportunities to contribute to community upliftment and social-impact activities.

Chapter 3: Who should consider applying?

This opportunity is aimed at young Africans who combine academic ability with a record—or a strong potential—of leadership, innovation and service to their communities.

It may be especially relevant for applicants who have a clear vision for using education to solve practical problems in areas such as employment, entrepreneurship, health, technology, education, climate action, public service or community development.

Before you apply

Check the admission requirements, available programmes and deadlines for your intended UCT faculty. Requirements can vary by programme and study level.

Chapter 5: How to apply

There are two separate applications. A Mastercard Foundation Scholars Program application is not the same as an academic application to UCT.

  1. Choose your UCT programme. Identify the undergraduate or postgraduate programme that fits your academic background and career direction.
  2. Apply for academic admission to UCT first. Submit your application for the degree programme through UCT’s admissions process.
  3. Apply to the Mastercard Foundation Scholars Program. Complete the scholarship application through the official programme process.
  4. Track faculty deadlines. For postgraduate study, confirm the specific requirements and deadline with the faculty offering your programme.
For undergraduate applicants, receiving an offer of admission from UCT is necessary before you can be eligible for the Scholars Program.

Chapter 6: Tips for a stronger application

  • Apply early and do not wait until the final deadline.
  • Choose a programme that connects clearly to your skills, experience and long-term goals.
  • Show evidence of academic commitment, leadership, volunteering, entrepreneurship or community service.
  • Explain a realistic problem you want to help solve in your community, country or Africa—and how your studies will help you do it.
  • Prepare your academic records and other required documents carefully, following the exact instructions from UCT and your faculty.
  • Use only official UCT and Mastercard Foundation Scholars Program information when applying. Never pay unofficial agents for a scholarship application.
